# Local market-data library

Open **Tools > Retain Hi-Res Data** in the app header. The clock lights green only after automatic downloads are enabled and saved. The tool window is modeless, so the main trading controls remain accessible.

## Choose equities

In **Equity lists**, create a named list and paste symbols separated by spaces, commas, or newlines. Connected lookups show company or fund names and reject unsupported symbols. Offline lists can be saved; identities are checked when downloading. Check individual equities and **Save list**. New lists show **SAVE LIST**; existing lists show **UPDATE LIST** only when there are unsaved changes. Checkbox changes, list names, list inclusion and membership edits count as changes. The button fades and slides into view, and disappears after saving or reverting to the saved values. Windows animation preferences are respected. Unchecking a member or removing a list does not delete downloaded history.

**Load Robinhood lists** retrieves accessible personal lists. Choose one and **Preview import**, then select individual equities and save a local copy. The app validates the returned item count and rejects incomplete or paginated responses it cannot finish. It resolves equity identities separately and never edits the Robinhood list. **Refresh source** previews additions and removals, preserving local exclusions; save to apply the preview. Repeating an import lets you keep multiple local lists.

**Export lists** appears when at least one saved list exists. It writes all saved list names, symbols and inclusion choices to a portable JSON file for backup or sharing with another PriceSentinel installation. Unsaved edits and candle data are not exported. Private Robinhood list and instrument IDs stay in the local configuration. **Import list file** adds copies with new local IDs.

## Schedule and download

In **Schedule & downloads**, choose the daily time in `HH:mm`, its time zone, and the library folder. Collection always includes all available regular, premarket, after-hours and overnight trading; there is no market-session setting. Press **Save schedule & folder**. The default is 13:15 in the computer's local zone. The saved zone follows daylight saving time; later computer-zone changes do not move it. A skipped clock time runs at the first valid minute; a repeated clock time runs once at the earlier occurrence.

Automatic collection requires the app to remain open and connected. It uses one deduplicated union of saved, enabled lists and included equities. At the chosen time it performs the same saved-history check as **Download gaps now**, including today's completed candles and earlier missing history. It requests all available trading hours, including overnight. Expected coverage follows the actual trading windows for the date and excludes weekend, holiday and early-close closures. The calendar includes recurring US exchange holidays and early closes; unscheduled closures are not predicted.

If authorization expires, use **Reconnect Robinhood** while trading and downloads are idle. This is an explicit login action; scheduled collection never opens a browser for authorization. Reconnecting is refused while a provider request is active.

Each scheduled run inspects the actual saved history separately for every included equity, starting with today's completed candles and then moving backward by date. Imported files and existing regular or extended files participate in the same checks; compatible saved candles are reused when expanding coverage to all available hours. Complete days require no broker request and do not stop the search for older gaps. Re-enabling downloads resumes from saved history, independently of when the schedule was enabled.

There is no fixed lookback window or assumed broker retention period. Older dates are checked progressively for available data. Previously recorded gaps beyond the current discovery boundary remain visible, but are not automatically queued again. A long outage can leave irrecoverable gaps. The app cannot recreate unavailable prices or guarantee a continuous candle for intervals with no reported trades.

Pending work, progress and older gap ranges survive restarts. Disconnected jobs wait for a connection; background requests cannot open a login prompt. Turn off and save automatic downloads to pause automatic queued work. This version does not download while the app is closed.

**Download gaps now** needs no dates. It uses the saved, included equities and library folder, checks their local files, and queues missing 15-second history across all available trading hours. Complete saved coverage is reused. Nearby missing ranges are combined into requests of at most six hours when the saved section between gaps is no more than five minutes. A response may include already-saved candles; identical candles are deduplicated, and newer valid price or volume corrections update the current daily file. Requests stay within actual trading windows and the captured cutoff. It includes today's completed candles through a captured 15-second boundary. A complete download of that current-day snapshot is labeled **Saved so far**, with its cutoff time; a later download can extend it. Forming candles and closed-market periods are excluded.

Every included equity is queued for today on each run, even when its captured coverage is already complete. All equities on that date finish before collection moves backward. Older dates are checked against saved candles and attempt records before queueing: complete coverage and ranges already attempted twice are skipped without adding a row. Eligible missing ranges use windows of up to one hour initially; once availability is established, remaining gaps use requests of up to six hours. Returned candles are saved immediately.

Collection continues backward past complete days and productive partial days whose remaining gaps have exhausted their two attempts. If an entire gap pass for an older regular trading day returns no broker candles, that equity stops immediately, even when its local file already contains candles. It does not repeat that empty pass or queue earlier dates. The daily JSON records this broker-unavailable boundary across restarts and later normal runs. Completed no-data checks in a queue saved by an older build are recovered before issuing more requests; superseded pending discovery entries are removed, while explicit manual work and saved candles are preserved. Other equities continue independently; the run ends when no equities remain to check. Today and overnight-only Sunday or holiday dates do not establish this boundary. Request errors and unchecked gaps never count as unavailable evidence. This is a stopping rule, not proof that every earlier session is unavailable.

Only eligible older stock/date work enters the queue. Expected **No data** discovery rows are hidden, while partial coverage and errors remain visible. Progress describes the work found so far as the queue grows. A new **Download gaps now** replaces prior availability rows for the included equities with a fresh check from today, preserving files and explicitly queued manual work. Requests remain strictly 15-second, serial, and rate limited. The shared date frontier, member tickers, and request cursors survive restarts and pauses. Pause is checked between local ticker/date scans as well as between requests. There is no separate Retry missing button.

The collector retains one library instance per folder. Scans reuse a bounded cache of validated file metadata; new or changed files are revalidated, and candle files selected for reading or merging are still fully checked. Stocks on the same date take turns between request batches. The main and miniature progress bars include partially checked days, while the checked-job count still counts finished days only.

The download table's **State** shows saved native 15-second candle coverage for the full trading day, excluding market closures. Overlapping saved periods count once; today's remaining trading hours still count in the denominator. This differs from the progress bar's checked-work percentage. **Details** identifies queued work, retry waits, saved gaps, unavailable data and errors. A failed attempt displays **0%** as an explicit failure indicator, while previously saved candles are kept. Unknown coverage displays **--** until local files are checked. Saved coverage survives restarts, and older retained jobs are populated from cached library metadata without broker requests.

Transport errors and timeouts, including provider cancellations not requested by the user, retry automatically up to two total attempts per request batch with a five-second delay. Dispatches count toward the older-range attempt cap, but failures do not establish unavailable history. An exhausted failed job pauses backward discovery; a new run rechecks eligibility, and **Forced download** is required once a gap has reached two attempts. A disconnected client keeps work pending until the connection returns; expired authentication can require reconnecting.
**Forced download** starts the same date-by-date discovery and bypasses recorded attempt caps and today's 15-minute cooldown. It can recheck a remembered empty-day boundary once; if the complete gap pass still returns no candles, that equity stops again. Actual broker returns clear the remembered boundary, including valid candles that are identical to saved data. Saved candles are reused, each response updates the daily JSON, and newly saved portions clear their attempt records. Normal pacing, cancellation, and transient retry limits still apply. Pausing or restarting preserves the forced run; later normal or scheduled runs respect the saved attempt records again.

In **Schedule & downloads**, click **Equity**, **Date**, or **State** to sort; click again to reverse direction. **State** sorts by its numeric coverage percentage. **Shift-click** another header to add a secondary sort or reverse that column while keeping the primary sort. Arrows show direction and numbers show precedence (**1**, then **2**). The initial order is newest date first, then equity A–Z. Sorting follows progress updates and survives switching tabs or reopening the window during the same app session.

**Clear**, beside **Forced download**, removes finished queue entries. It is disabled while any download is active or queued, including paused work and pending retries, and when the queue is empty. Clearing preserves saved daily files, remembered empty ranges, continuity records and the download schedule.

Each day's JSON contains an optional `collection` section alongside its candles. It records compact unavailable ranges and per-range attempt counts, scoped to the broker, instrument, date, session, and adjustment identity. A dispatch is recorded before calling the broker, so restarting cannot reset a gap's allowance. Successful responses record every unresolved part of the requested span, including holes in partial responses. When candles are saved, only their covered portions are removed from the attempt and unavailable records. A wholly empty day can have a zero-candle JSON record. `brokerHistoryUnavailableAtUtc` records a completed unproductive older-day pass; `lastBrokerCandlesAtUtc` records actual positive broker responses so importing an older copy cannot restore a cleared boundary. Reading existing local candles does not erase the broker boundary.

Unavailable ranges on the current Eastern date use a 15-minute cooldown. Today remains queued on each run so newly completed candles can be collected; it is exempt from the historical two-attempt cap. On older dates, each unresolved range has at most two normal attempts. Errors and interrupted requests can consume an attempt, but only validated successful responses establish unavailable evidence. **Forced download** bypasses these restrictions.

Copying a daily JSON carries its collection record with it; no SQLite gap database is required. Older JSON without this optional metadata remains readable and learns attempt records as downloads run. The legacy SQLite cache is left untouched; its entries are not imported because they do not record how many attempts were made. Collection metadata is excluded from the candle `DatasetHash`, so recording an attempt does not alter Replay pins or create a new candle archive. Saved candles remain unchanged when the broker returns no usable data.

The same compact download activity panel stays visible across all three tabs, keeping its font sizes, progress bar and tab position steady. It names the current stock and date while checking saved files, waiting for the broker, or saving candles. A busy spinner remains visible during active work. While checking older dates before queueing them, the heading says **Checking older history** and both progress bars animate instead of appearing finished, even when the retained queue is fully checked. The indicator uses reserved space so the information button and layout stay steady. Hover over **i** beside the heading for the current request details, elapsed time, background-download guidance, schedule help and gap-handling explanations. Ready batches continue immediately, with serial requests spaced at least 250 milliseconds apart; there is no fixed pause between batches. Retry deadlines and connection waits have their own explanations. The progress bar counts completed checks across the retained queue, including unavailable or partial results. Separate counts distinguish complete files from items needing attention. The current request range is shown in Eastern time alongside the percentage of that stock/date's requested trading time checked. This measures checking progress, not saved-data coverage or completion of an in-flight broker response. Current-step elapsed time and Last progress update as each section finishes, even before the entire date finishes.

Hover over or click **[i]** beside the download status to open its information panel. The panel stays open while you scroll or drag its scrollbar. Use **×**, press Escape, or click outside to close the panel.

Closing the retention window leaves downloads running in the background while PriceSentinel stays open. A compact progress bar inside **Retain Hi-Res Data** shows the current queue progress; its tooltip shows the latest status. Click it during collection to open **Schedule & downloads** with the current job states. Pausing retains the queue; closing the whole app stops collection and preserves pending work for the next launch.

**Pause downloads** cancels the current request and keeps queued work stopped until **Resume downloads** or an app restart. Saved files and queued jobs are preserved. **Cancel connection** interrupts an explicit login request. Closing this tools window leaves background collection running; closing PriceSentinel stops it. You can change tabs, scroll, inspect rows, and edit drafts while downloading. Save/import actions that conflict with active collection are disabled until idle. Updates change existing job rows in place, preserving selection and scrolling. The saved schedule is labeled separately, and **UNSAVED CHANGES** appears when edited schedule/folder settings have not yet been applied.

Retention downloads request **only genuine, completed 15-second candles** across all available trading hours. A complete saved session is reused without downloading again. Compatible candles from older regular or extended files are reused when filling the surrounding trading hours. Each request covers only a missing section, with a maximum of six hours (1,440 candles). Native responses are merged into one current 15-second file per stock and Eastern calendar date. Matching overlaps are deduplicated; new candles fill gaps or extend the day. Long stretches of complete coverage are skipped. Newer valid overlapping prices and positive volumes replace saved values, including lower positive revisions. Zero, null, or empty fields keep existing values; incomplete new price bars without saved counterparts are omitted. If filling an empty field would produce inconsistent OHLC, the saved price bar is kept together. Revision-only responses are saved even when they add no candles. Timing, provenance, duplicate and negative-value checks still apply before the daily file is atomically replaced, with the previous snapshot archived by hash. Empty responses preserve saved candles and leave the missing range unresolved. These actions fill gaps rather than refreshing all saved history. There is no 30-second or 60-second fallback in this downloader. Replay retains its separate, explicitly labeled coarse-data fallback.

## Portable history

The default root is `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PriceSentinel3000\MarketData`. The folder can be changed when a download is not active. For example:

```text
MarketData/
  README.md
  2026/
    08 - August/
      NFLX/
        2026-08-24.15s.json
    09 - September/
      SOXL/
        2026-09-04.15s.json
```

Only needed folders are created. Month names are fixed English with numeric prefixes. Daily grouping uses `America/New_York`; each candle retains exact UTC start, end and availability times. Schema 1 accepts finalized candles available at source close. Fetch time is separate.

Each self-contained UTF-8 JSON document includes schema version, public instrument identity, provider, actual interval, adjustment policy/basis, requested coverage, gaps and an immutable SHA-256 dataset hash. Prices and volumes are invariant decimal strings. Unknown volume is `null`; complete price coverage does not imply complete OHLCV. The library's generated README describes these rules and canonical hashing.

Copy a day file, ticker folder, month or year into another library, then **Rescan library**. Each current 15-second day is self-contained: no database, credentials or sidecars are required to replay it. Rescan consolidates compatible legacy sections into the canonical date.15s.json file. Saves and consolidation use a cross-process library write lock, validate the combined candles, archive superseded exact-hash documents under .archive, and atomically replace the current day before removing old section paths. Identical saves do not rewrite the day. Normal scans exclude the archive; exact-hash reads and pinned queries still find prior snapshots there. Copy the entire library including .archive if prior Replay hashes must remain resolvable. Incompatible provenance, invalid candle timing or values, or a damaged destination leaves the originals available and reports the problem. Other native resolutions retain their separate files. Individual files remain size-limited and validated; there is no automatic history expiration.

Daily files use half-open ranges based on each candle START in America/New_York: a candle ending at midnight belongs to the preceding date, and the candle starting at midnight belongs to the next. UTC timestamps preserve order across daylight-saving changes and month/year boundaries. Joining consecutive daily files preserves every saved candle without adding an artificial boundary gap. Actual missing broker candles remain explicit gaps; consolidation does not invent them.

Robinhood supplies split-adjusted data without an explicit adjustment revision. Files disclose this as `robinhood-split-unversioned`; hashes pin the exact prices fetched. Different provider/instrument/adjustment identities cannot be silently combined. Compatible 15-second daily consolidation uses the newest fetch for overlapping price and volume revisions while retaining saved values for zero or missing fields. Incoming saves win equal fetch times; imported-file consolidation uses the lexicographically lowest hash for ties. Coarser revisions and explicit query/pin selection keep their existing behavior. Revision selection and hash pinning controls are not shown in Local library.

The **Local library** table shows one row per equity and Eastern calendar date, newest dates first. Compatible saved chunks are consolidated on disk, counting each covered candle interval once. Each current 15-second day occupies one file; archived snapshots stay out of the table. **Day coverage** uses the widest session represented in those files: all hours, extended, or regular. Expected candle counts exclude closed-market periods and account for early closes. Hover over the percentage for unique saved and expected counts, session scope and saved file count. For today, the percentage compares saved candles with trading candles completed up to the current time, excluding the still-forming candle, future hours and market closures. It can reach 100% before midnight. Past dates use their full completed session. Before any candles are due, coverage shows **--**. The percentage refreshes from cached metadata as time advances; the **Candles** column still shows the total saved count for the Eastern-dated file. This measures saved price-candle coverage; Replay separately checks whether overlapping prices, volume and provenance agree. Mixed resolutions or incompatible source identities remain one row with unavailable totals and an explanation, rather than misleading combined counts. Original files and hashes are preserved. The table fills the available space, with a compact background-download summary above it. Revision hashes and Replay pin controls are not displayed. After a rescan, the total size of active saved candle files appears in MB above the table, aligned to its right edge (1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes). Archived snapshots and other supporting files are excluded. Scan results show a short count in the footer; full notices are available in the collapsed **Library details** panel, which scrolls within a bounded height.

Click a **Local library** column header to sort; click it again to reverse direction. **Shift-click** another header to add a secondary sort or reverse its direction while keeping the primary sort. For equity groups with dates inside each group, choose **Equity**, then Shift-click **Date**. Arrows show direction, and the numbers show precedence (**1**, then **2**). The initial order is newest date first, then equity A–Z. The chosen sort survives rescans and reopening the window during the same app session.

Click a **Local library** row (or select it and press Enter) to open its saved-coverage timeline. The timeline preserves the row's **Eastern calendar date** and shows its boundaries with actual dates and times in the computer's local time zone. For example, September 10 Eastern runs from September 9 at 21:00 through September 10 at 21:00 Pacific daylight time. At 23:38 Pacific on September 9, the completed overnight portion is already elapsed; only later candles are future. Adjacent Eastern dates are not combined to build a local calendar day. Stocks confirmed as 24-hour eligible show the full Eastern day, including its actual length across daylight-saving transitions; other confirmed stocks show the 04:00–20:00 Eastern extended-hours span converted to local time. Eligibility uses the broker's cached 24 Hour Market list through an existing connection, without login or candle downloads. If it cannot be confirmed, the full Eastern day is shown with an explanation.

Each 15-minute block is green when all completed 15-second candles are saved, light green when some are saved, and black when none are saved. The market calendar still accounts for closures and early closes within the displayed span. Market closures are striped gray and future time is solid blue, matching the legend; neither counts as missing. Click a 15-minute block to highlight it and show its local time range, saved count, missing count and coverage in the selected-block panel beneath the legend. Selection stays visible until another block is chosen or the dialog is closed. Both the timeline and Local library percentage exclude future candles. Their counts can still differ: the timeline uses confirmed session eligibility and native 15-second candles, while the table uses the sessions and native intervals represented in saved files and cannot combine incompatible sources. Click outside the dialog, press Escape, or use **×** to close it.

Selecting a black **Missing** block shows a small **DOWNLOAD** button inside the top-right corner of the full-width selected-block panel. It retries that block and touching Missing blocks on either side, stopping at complete, partial, closed or future blocks. Requests include only completed 15-second candles and retry remembered empty ranges within that span. The selected local interval is split into Eastern daily files when necessary; saved candles are preserved and new candles merge into those files. This action does not start older-day discovery. The button is disabled while downloads or queued work remain. Closing the dialog lets the background request continue. When an attempt finishes, the library and open timeline refresh, retaining the selected block. Status stays at the bottom-left inside the panel and distinguishes unavailable data from a failed request or queued retry. Selecting another block clears the status, including messages from an earlier download that finishes afterward.

## Replay and analysis

In the dashboard's **Data timing** panel, enter a Replay date and press **Enter**, press **CHECK**, or choose a day with the calendar button. The check uses the current symbol, local start/end times, saved session bounds, library folder, offline choice and revision pins. It does not start Replay or write candles to disk. Changing these inputs invalidates the previous check.

The date field and calendar use these colors, with text labels and tooltips:

| Color | Availability for the entire selected time range |
| --- | --- |
| Dark green / 15D | Complete genuine 15-second data on disk |
| Light green / 15B | Complete genuine 15-second data verified with Robinhood |
| Orange / 30–60s | Coarser 30- or 60-second data; the status gives the exact interval |
| Red / 2m | Two-minute data on disk; this adapter cannot request two-minute candles from Robinhood |
| Gray / GAP, ?, or — | Partial coverage, not checked, or no returned history; see the explanation |

Opening or paging the calendar scans local files without querying the broker for every day. Selecting a date checks the broker as needed, using an existing connection; it never opens a login prompt. Broker day colors are cached for up to five minutes. Coarse local calendar colors identify what is saved, and their tooltips prompt a selected-date check for finer broker data. There is no assumed age cutoff: successful responses containing synthesized/interpolated placeholders do not count as genuine source coverage.

Broker verification reads the selected range into memory because the API has no separate availability endpoint. **START** reuses the checked candles, saves the selected broker result, or rereads the exact checked disk hashes, then constructs the Replay stream. It does not request the same checked broker data twice. Missing or modified pinned files fail rather than silently selecting another revision. A changed dashboard selection requires new data selection. An explicit check prefers complete coverage, trying the supported resolutions in order; partial history remains clearly labeled if no complete source is found. Script interval compatibility is still validated at START.

START and the availability check use the same disk-first selection. Replay reads saved 15-, 30-, 60- and 120-second data before making broker requests. It combines compatible saved pieces whose overlapping candles agree. If coverage remains incomplete, it requests the span containing the gaps at genuine 15-second, then 30-second, then 1-minute resolution. Saved candles have priority when the same native interval is returned again. The Robinhood adapter has no native 2-minute request; genuine imported 2-minute files can be used, but unavailable prices are never invented.

When filling a gap requires a coarser bar, Replay uses that whole bar and aggregates complete finer spans upward to a uniform Replay interval. It never splits a coarse bar or counts overlapping bars twice. Remaining gaps stay visible. The source-data label and session/MCP provenance identify combined history, its native resolutions and exact source hashes. Files retain their native candles; only the in-memory Replay stream is aggregated. A strategy must support the resulting Replay interval, and its tested interval is never silently changed. Prices, signals, risk checks and simulated fills become available only at the completed Replay candle's close.

Before START, choose **Replay from local files only** in the dashboard's Replay settings to prevent broker history requests. It is off by default and locked during a session. Changing it invalidates the previous availability check. **Use offline** on the welcome screen opens the app without login. Ordinary saved coverage is combined automatically when overlaps agree. Unconsolidated or coarser revisions with conflicting prices or volume still require explicit selection; the 15-second save/consolidation policy does not change query or pin rules. The MCP library tools can read a particular dataset by hash for reproducible analysis. Local library does not expose Replay pinning or revision-policy controls.

The companion MCP server adds two read-only tools, usable without an active session or authentication:

- `library_datasets(offset, limit, catalogHash)`: validated dataset metadata and bounded diagnostics. Save the returned catalog hash and pass it on subsequent pages; a changed catalog requires restarting pagination.
- `library_candles(datasetHash, offset, limit)`: exact immutable candles, coverage and provenance, up to 100 rows per page. Prices are decimal strings and unavailable volume stays null.

These tools read only the configured library and accept no arbitrary filesystem path. Both are also safe to inspect while LIVE is selected. Collection never submits orders or changes the app's selected trading strategy.
