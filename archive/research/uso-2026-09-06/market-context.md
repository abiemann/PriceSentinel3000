# USO fund structure and dated context

United States Oil Fund, LP (USO) is a NYSE Arca commodity pool. Its daily NAV
objective concerns a WTI crude-oil futures benchmark; it does not simply own
physical oil or shares of oil producers. Futures holdings, monthly contract
rolls, and market price versus NAV can affect tracking. The experiment therefore
uses USO's own reported share-price history, without substituting WTI futures or
spot oil observations. That choice is a research inference from the fund's
structure, not a prediction of price direction.

USCF states that USO changed its portfolio roll from ten days to five days
on January 1, 2026 without changing its benchmark or objective. Exact September
roll dates were not verified, so this report
does not label any tested session a roll day.
